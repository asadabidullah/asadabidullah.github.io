Land Claiming

How to protect your builds so nobody can mess with them.

We use the GriefPrevention plugin for our SMP. It's incredibly easy to use and automatically keeps your stuff safe. Here's how it works:

Golden Shovel Tool

Right-click opposite corners of your desired land with a golden shovel to create rectangular claims.

Auto Chest Protection

Placing a chest automatically claims and protects the surrounding blocks for new players.

Claim Block Accrual

Earn allowance blocks passively as you play on the server, growing your building limits over time.

Subdivisions

Large claims can be broken into smaller sub-areas using the sub-claim tools.

Investigation Tool

Right-clicking blocks with a stick shows who owns the claim and when they were last online.
