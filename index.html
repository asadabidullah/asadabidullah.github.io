Guidelines

Server Rules

Simple rules to keep things chill for everyone. Break 'em and you're out.

1

Be Respectful

No harassment, discrimination, racism, sexism, or toxic behavior. Treat others how you'd want to be treated.

2

No Griefing or Stealing

Don't touch what isn't yours. Unclaimed builds are still off-limits. Use the claiming system.

3

No Hacking or Exploiting

X-ray, fly hacks, speed hacks, duping glitches — all bannable. Report bugs instead of abusing them.

4

No Spam or Self-Promotion

Don't flood chat, send unsolicited links, or promote other servers/Discords without permission.

5

Follow Discord ToS

No NSFW content, no underage users under 13, no doxxing. Standard Discord rules apply at all times.

6

Listen to Staff

Staff decisions are final. If you have an issue, appeal through the proper channels — don't argue in chat.
